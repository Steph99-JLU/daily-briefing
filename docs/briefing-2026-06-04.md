## AI & RESEARCH

**Meta delays its most capable model indefinitely** — Muse Spark, reportedly competitive with OpenAI and Anthropic's top models, has no scheduled developer release and has not been evaluated externally. *Why it matters:* With ~$65B in AI capex committed, Meta cannot sustain a reputation gap if competitor releases continue unimpeded; this delay signals either capability shortfall or internal process friction. **Google Gemma 4 12B: multimodal, runs on 16GB laptop** — Encoder-free model handling text, vision, and native audio, claiming near-parity with Gemma 4 26B MoE. Available on Kaggle and Hugging Face. *Why it matters:* Continued push of capable open weights to edge hardware narrows the moat for cloud-only inference, accelerating local-first AI deployment. (sources: TLDR AI, TLDR Dev, TLDR main)

**OpenAI Codex discovers CVE-2026-49975 (HTTP/2 "Bomb")** — Codex independently identified a remote DoS chaining an HPACK indexed-reference bomb with a zero-byte flow-control stall, affecting nginx, Apache, Microsoft IIS, Envoy, and Cloudflare Pingora in their default HTTP/2 configurations. A single client can force ~32GB server allocation in ~20 seconds on Apache/Envoy. *Why it matters:* An LLM tool doing original security research — not just assisting humans — and finding a critical vulnerability in near-universal production infrastructure is a meaningful capability signal. Patch immediately: nginx 1.29.8+, Apache mod_http2 v2.0.41. (source: TLDR InfoSec)

**AI worm demo (U of Toronto): spreads via known CVEs, hijacks compute** — Researchers built a worm on a single-GPU open-weight model that searches the KEV catalog, adapts around partial patches, and hijacks compute resources as it propagates. Cannot discover zero-days — but the existing KEV backlog is large enough to matter. *Why it matters:* Raises the operational minimum for AI-aware patching cadences and network segmentation. (source: TLDR InfoSec)

**LLMs vs. deliberately vulnerable app: GPT-5.5 wins, Claude Sonnet budget-capped** — Developer tested $1,500 worth of LLM runs against a vulnerable book review app. GPT-5.5: 7/10. DeepSeek-V4-Pro: 3/10. Claude Sonnet 4.6: 2 successes, 5 token-cap failures. *Why it matters:* GPT-5.5's lead on offensive security tasks is a concrete data point for red teams and enterprise procurement. (source: TLDR AI)

**Quantum ECDSA timeline advancing: AI agent swarms at 80% of Google's target** — Eigen Labs researchers reached a Toffoli gate-qubit product of 6.5B using AI agent swarms, vs. Google's undisclosed ~3B target and a prior published best of 24.5B. *Why it matters:* Every 6 months this gets meaningfully closer. Organizations should be actively accelerating post-quantum migration, not monitoring. (source: TLDR Crypto)

**Google "Sleep" paradigm for continual LLM learning** — Proposes consolidating short-term in-context knowledge into model weights via distillation/replay, plus a "Dreaming" stage using RL for synthetic self-improvement curricula. *Why it matters:* If scalable, this significantly lowers the cost of keeping deployed models current between training runs. (source: TLDR AI)

**Fei-Fei Li taxonomy of world models** — Detailed breakdown of the term across computer vision, robotics, RL, and generative AI: renderers (visual observations), simulators (physical state), planners (action generation). *Why it matters:* Definitional clarity is needed — the term is being used to justify divergent roadmaps from embodied robotics to pure reasoning systems. (sources: TLDR AI, TLDR Dev)

## ANALYST TAKE

- **The AI IPO window is the sector's first real pricing moment.** Anthropic at $965B with $50B ARR implies a ~19x forward P/S — defensible only if revenue compounds at 2–3x annually for 3+ years. SpaceX in the same window means capital markets will be asked to absorb two historically-unprecedented valuations simultaneously. Any disappointment in either will reset expectations for every private AI company.

- **Enterprise AI cost governance has arrived, and it will hurt tool vendors.** Uber's cap, Gartner's efficiency mandate, Goldman's cost warnings, and Microsoft's per-outcome benchmark all mark the same inflection: the "experiment with everything" phase is over. Vendors selling on capability alone, without clear ROI metrics, are now exposed.

- **Agent security governance is 12–18 months behind agent deployment.** Workday's Agent Passport, Cisco's AgenticOps, Anthropic's containment post, the AI worm demo, and the "AI agents are the new insiders" framing all point to the same structural gap. Organizations deploying agents without governance tooling are accumulating operational risk they cannot yet measure.

- **Codex independently discovering CVE-2026-49975 is a directional shift.** This is not AI assisting human security researchers — it is AI doing original security research on its own and finding a critical vulnerability in near-universal production infrastructure. The acceleration of AI-driven vulnerability discovery will outpace remediation capacity unless patching processes are restructured.

- **Stablecoin settlement is becoming critical infrastructure whether regulators are ready or not.** Mastercard, Deel, Revolut, Coinbase, and FDIC-insured bank rails all moved in a single week. The question is no longer "will stablecoins scale?" but "which stablecoin rails become the default backbone for global payments?"
